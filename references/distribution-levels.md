# Distribution Automation Levels

## Level 0: Founder Performs

The founder identifies, contacts, and speaks with people. The agent may prepare
notes but must not replace relationship-building.

## Level 1: Agent Prepares

Unlock after at least one valid repetition. The agent may research prospects,
draft messages, and prepare posts. The founder sends and handles replies.

## Level 2: Agent Operates With Approval

Unlock after repeated signal from a specific audience, message, and channel.
The agent may maintain lists and follow-ups. Every external send requires
founder approval.

## Level 3: Agent Operates Proven Campaigns

Unlock only after a campaign repeatedly produces qualified conversations or
commitments. Require explicit caps, stop conditions, review cadence, and easy
shutdown. Revert a level when response quality degrades.

Useful optional primitives at Levels 2-3: lead pipeline, outreach pipeline,
CRM, and social growth strategy. Their presence does not justify unlocking.
