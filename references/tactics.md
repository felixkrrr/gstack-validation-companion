# Validation Tactics

Choose the cheapest tactic that reaches the right people and tests the current
uncertainty. Early work is founder-led.

## Trusted Validation Circle

Build durable relationships with a small number of credible ICP contacts who
understand the workflow and will plainly say when an idea is bad, irrelevant,
or low priority. After each rep, assess ICP fit, domain expertise, candor,
evidence specificity, and willingness to continue.

Prefer re-engaging credible contacts when showing a meaningfully changed
hypothesis, framing, or offer. Do not repeatedly ask them the same question.
When testing market generality, a new audience, or whether the message works
beyond existing relationships, include at least one fresh ICP contact. A
trusted circle speeds learning; it must not become an echo chamber.

## Warm Network First

1. Search phone contacts for likely users or introducers.
2. Send a relevant WhatsApp broadcast asking for introductions.
3. Search LinkedIn first-degree connections by role.
4. Warm second-degree connections before asking for input.
5. Publish a network post about the problem and ask for introductions.
6. End conversations with: "Who else do you know who deals with this?"

## Direct Outreach

1. Find businesses through targeted web search, identify the responsible
   operator, and contact them directly.
2. Use trigger events such as funding, promotions, hiring, or job changes.
3. Mine competitor reviews and comments for people already experiencing the
   problem.

## In Person

Visit local businesses, attend meetups or trade shows, spend time where the
target group works, or offer a short useful demo to a class or community.
Optimize for conversations, not attendee counts.

## Communities

Ask genuine problem questions in relevant Reddit, Slack, Discord, Facebook, or
professional communities. Contribute before sharing an offer. Treat replies as
potential conversations, not engagement metrics.

## Recurring Problem Posts

This tactic is opt-in. Publish specific observations, unresolved questions,
experiment results, and changed beliefs. Never publish private customer
details. Track conversations and introductions caused by posts, not reach.
